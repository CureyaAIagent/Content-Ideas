Your agent should work like this:

### STEP 1: DATA INGESTION

- YouTube → trending videos + search suggestions
- Google Trends → rising keywords

### STEP 2: TOPIC EXTRACTION

### STEP 3: VIRALITY SCORING ENGINE

This is where most people fail.

#### 📊 PARAMETERS (VERY IMPORTANT)

Your Trend Agent must evaluate each topic on:

🔥 1. VIEW VELOCITY
How fast views are increasing

Why:
Trending = speed, not total views

🔥 2. SEARCH MOMENTUM
Google Trends curve

Score:
Rising = High
Flat = Medium
Declining = Reject

🔥 3. COMPETITION DENSITY

Check:
How many videos already exist

Insight:
If top creators haven't posted yet → GOLD opportunity

🔥 4. CONTENT GAP

Ask:
Is there confusion?
Is explanation missing?

👉 Confusion = viral explainer opportunity

🔥 5. EMOTIONAL TRIGGER SCORE

High viral emotions:
- Fear
- Curiosity
- Power
- Money
- Identity

🔥 6. CLICKABILITY (CTR POTENTIAL)

Check:
Can this become a strong title?

Example:
❌ "Psychology study on habits"
✅ "Why rich people never do this"

🔥 7. WATCHTIME POTENTIAL

Ask:
Can this hold attention for 8–10 minutes?

🔥 8. FORMAT FIT

Classify:
- Shorts
- Long-form
- Documentary

🔥 9. REPETITION SIGNAL

If same topic appears:
- YouTube
- Google Trends
- Reddit

👉 = CONFIRMED TREND

🔥 10. TIMING WINDOW

Trend lifecycle:
- Reddit → Day 1
- Google Trends → Day 2–3
- YouTube → Day 3–7

👉 Your agent must catch Day 1–2
